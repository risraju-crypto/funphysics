Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FunPhysics ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our educational physics learning application.

The App is developed and maintained by Raju N (GitHub: risraju-crypto).

1. Information We Collect

FunPhysics is designed with privacy in mind. We collect the minimum data necessary for the app to function:

  • Local Progress Data: Points, levels, completed labs, quiz scores, and achievements are stored locally on your device using the browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any server.
  • No Personal Accounts: The App does not require user registration, login, or creation of accounts. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Device Information: Like most web-based apps, basic technical information (such as browser type and device type) may be processed by your browser or hosting infrastructure, but we do not actively collect or store this information.

2. How We Use Information

The local progress data is used solely to:

  • Track your learning progress within the App
  • Display your points, levels, and achievements
  • Provide a personalized experience on your device

We do not use any data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing purposes.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user data to third parties.

The only external resource the App may load is Google Fonts (for typography). Google Fonts may process limited technical information according to Google's Privacy Policy. No user progress or personal data is shared with Google.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All progress data is stored only on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing your browser/app data for the FunPhysics site/app
  • Uninstalling the App
  • Using your device's storage management tools

Once deleted from your device, the data cannot be recovered by us because we never possessed it.

5. Children's Privacy

FunPhysics is an educational app suitable for students, including those under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App stores only local progress data and requires no accounts or personal details, it is designed to be safe for younger users. Parents can clear local data at any time as described above.

6. Security

Because all user progress data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers, the risk of unauthorized access by us is eliminated. We recommend keeping your device secure and using up-to-date browsers.

7. Third-Party Services

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may log standard access information (IP addresses, request times) for security and operational purposes. We do not control or access these logs.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
